Exposure, population & economic inputs

Mortality is estimated with the Global Exposure Mortality Model (GEMM): a non-linear hazard-ratio curve is fit to concentration, then applied to the baseline death rate of the adult (25+) population. Value of Statistical Life scales with each country's GDP per capita.
